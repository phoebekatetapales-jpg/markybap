From carinderia to kitchen

About Markybap

One phone. One spoon.
Zero sugarcoating.

It started with a cheap phone tripod and a plate of tapsilog at a roadside carinderia. I just wanted to tell my friends which spots were sulit — and which ones weren't. Ang dami palang nakikinig.

Hundreds of food trips later, the community kept asking the same thing: "Ikaw, Marky, anong luto mo?" So I went back to my lola's recipes, tested them like I test everyone else's, and only bottled the ones that scored a 9 or higher.

Same rule for my reviews and my food: if it's not good, I'll say it. If it's good, I'll sell it.

The Markybap Scale

How I Rate

Every score out of 10 is built from three pillars. No paid ratings — sponsored videos are always labeled.

01

Taste & Authenticity

Does it taste like it should — or better? Seasoning, texture, freshness, and respect for the original recipe.

40% of the score

02

Value for Money

Sulit ba? Portion, price, and quality weighed together. A ₱60 silog can beat a ₱600 steak.

30% of the score

03

Crave Factor

Will I think about it at 2 AM? Would I drive back for it? The "balik-balikan" test.

30% of the score
